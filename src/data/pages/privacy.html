Privacy policy

Worcester Hydro Jetting Pros helps people request hydro jetting information and connect with service providers in Worcester. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used. If you have a question about your information, call {{PHONE_DISPLAY}}.

Information you provide

When you submit a request, the form asks for your name, phone number, an optional email address and a description of the drain issue. We use these details to review and respond to your request and help connect you with a hydro jetting service provider. Your request details may be shared with providers who can follow up about service. Please do not send financial, medical or other sensitive information through the form.

Forms and analytics

The website uses an AirChatty tracking and form script to process requests through a GoHighLevel customer relationship management system. Technical information about your visit, such as your browser, device and pages viewed, may also be collected by the website and its service providers. Google Analytics is not currently configured with a working measurement ID on this site. If analytics is enabled later, this policy will be updated to describe it.

Your choices

You can call instead of using the form. To ask about information you submitted or request access, a correction or deletion, call {{PHONE_DISPLAY}}. We will review requests under applicable law and any recordkeeping requirements. If this site's services or data practices change, this policy may be updated here.
